I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at The University of Alabama. My research interests focus on software security,peer code review, empirical software engineering,and AI-assisted secure software development.Before beginning my doctoral studies, I served as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Dhaka University of Engineering and Technology, Bangladesh. My research examines how peer code review practices influence security vulnerability detection in open-source software. I develop data-driven and AI-based approaches, including large language models, to provide context-aware support for security-focused code review.
