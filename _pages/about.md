I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at The University of Alabama. My research focuses on software security, peer code review, and the use of large language models to support developers during security-focused code review.Before beginning my doctoral studies, I served as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Dhaka University of Engineering and Technology. My current research examines how software developers identify security issues during code review, what information they need, and how context-aware tools can improve the review process.
